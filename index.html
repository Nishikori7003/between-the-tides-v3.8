3.8 Web Edition · Rules

Both sides start at 0 points; ecology starts at 3. After 5 effective rounds, each side needs 17 points to succeed. Reaching the target early does not end the game.

Tides and actions

High tide: A or Wait. Mid tide: A/B. Low tide: A/B/C. Each round, draw from a deck with two cards of each tide, then replace the card and shuffle.

Choose secretly, then reveal

Choose an area secretly and ring the Decision Bell to lock it. Reveal both choices together, then the human chooses light or heavy harvesting. The bird draws one token first; the human draws afterward. Draw blindly without replacement.

Scoring and ecology

Base points per token: A=1, B=3, C=5. Preferred food gives the bird +2; the human has no preference bonus. Light harvest: 1 token. Heavy harvest: 2 tokens and ecology -1. Sharing an area costs another 1 ecology but no bird energy.

If both Wait at high tide, ecology recovers by 1 (maximum 3). No effective round is counted and resources are not replenished. If only one side Waits, the round counts and ecology does not recover.

Shared failure takes priority

Check after every draw. If an area has no tokens of any type left, both sides immediately fail: stop drawing and do not credit pending points this round. Ecology reaching 0 also causes immediate shared failure. Running out of one food type does not empty an area.

After five effective rounds: both reaching 17 means shared success; if only one reaches 17, only that side succeeds. If neither reaches 17, both fail their score targets.

Resources and birds

Starting quantities (shellfish/worms/crabs): A 3/4/5, B 4/5/3, C 5/3/4. Map labels show fixed starting amounts, not remaining resources. History and exports retain full resource data. Oystercatcher prefers shellfish; Curlew prefers worms; Redshank prefers crabs.

Local AI and history

The AI uses only public state and history, locking its area before you choose. The human AI picks its harvest amount after the reveal. It cannot predict random draws. Copy or export history before refreshing, which clears the current game.
