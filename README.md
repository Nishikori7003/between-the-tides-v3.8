# Between the Tides — 3.8 English Guided Edition

Extract the entire ZIP, open the between-the-tides folder, and double-click index.html. Use Chrome or Edge. No server, installation, internet connection or online AI service is needed.

Choose Bird or Human. Enable or disable guidance at the start or during play. Initial food quantities are shown; remaining quantities are kept in the detailed history and exports for analysis.

Keyboard: Tab/Shift+Tab moves focus. 1/2/3 selects A/B/C; 4 selects Wait when legal. After the reveal, 1/2 chooses light/heavy harvesting. Enter/Space confirms or continues. Esc closes dialogs. A/B/C/W also work.

When you scroll below the main dashboard, a floating bar shows tide, ecology and effective rounds. Drag its handle, or focus it and use arrow keys; Home resets its position. Click the information to return to the dashboard.

Music starts after a click or key press, subject to browser playback permissions. Volume settings control BGM, bell, click and waves separately. Timers remind you to choose; they do not force a choice. Player statistics are stored locally in this browser. Export records before moving files or clearing browser data. English statistics use a separate storage key.

This edition translates the current Chinese 3.8 package, retaining its gameplay, AI, guidance, audio and controls. It is a separate copy and does not replace the Chinese edition. Web edition 3.8 is based on the 3.75 playtest rules and agreed rulings. Open How to play for the full in-game rules.
